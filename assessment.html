Why Driver Assessment Matters

A driver assessment helps determine your level of competence, confidence, road awareness, and vehicle handling skills.

Our instructors evaluate your driving ability and recommend the most suitable training program based on your performance.

Whether you're a beginner, returning driver, or preparing for your driving test, our assessment ensures you start at the right level.

What We Assess

Our assessment program covers both practical driving skills and road safety awareness.

Vehicle Control

Steering, clutch control, braking, parking, and maneuvering.

Road Awareness

Traffic observation, signs, junctions, and lane discipline.

Defensive Driving

Hazard anticipation, alertness, and safe driving habits.

Highway Code

Knowledge of traffic rules and road sign interpretation.
